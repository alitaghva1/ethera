## Logline

A single mage descends through a corrupted crypt whose magical
containment has failed. Beat the keepers, chain status combos, and
earn permanent bindings with every death.

## Touchstones

- **Hades** — moment-to-moment combat feel, room-as-location reading
- **The Binding of Isaac** — relic density, item-combination surprise
- **Noita** — status-combo chemistry, environmental material storytelling

## What makes Ethera distinct

- **Status combos cascade by themselves.** Burn + slow always
  shatters. Burning + knockback always scatters embers. Slow + crit
  always petrifies. Six combos so far, more coming. Combos are not
  unlocks — they're physics.
- **Spell modifiers are relics, not slots.** Find Echo Quill and
  every blast echoes. Pick up Split Cinder and every third cast
  fans embers. Stack them. They compose.
- **The room reads as a chamber, not a test arena.** Material
  storytelling pass (acid stains, blood smears, fungal patches,
  corruption cracks, wall seepage trails) makes each room feel
  inhabited and recently damaged.
