## App name

**Ethera: Beneath the Ruin**

## Short description (≤300 chars)

A top-down dark-fantasy roguelite. Descend through a corrupted crypt
whose magical containment has failed — chain status combos, fold
spell modifiers, and earn permanent bindings each death. Built for
Hades-tier feel, Binding-of-Isaac synergy density, and Noita-flavor
chemistry.

## About this game (long description)

ETHERA is a top-down 2D roguelite about a single mage's descent into a
fortress where the magic itself has gone wrong. Cursed material has
leaked from the alchemical fonts. Floors burn, freeze, and bleed.
Every room is a chamber with a story you can read in the stains.

### Pillars

- **Hades-tier moment-to-moment feel.** Dash-cancel, parry chime,
  white-flash hit silhouette, trauma-based camera shake, tier-scaled
  damage feedback.
- **Binding of Isaac density.** 59 relics across four tiers (common /
  rare / legendary / mythic). 4 active relics with distinct verbs
  (defensive teleport, burning ward, risk/tempo buff, AoE burst).
  4 spell modifiers that compose with everything (echo, split-ember,
  gravity drag, chain runes).
- **Noita-flavor chemistry.** Six status combos that fire automatically
  when conditions stack: SHATTER (burn + slow), KINDLE_SPREAD (burn +
  death), PETRIFY (slow + crit), SCATTER_FLAMES (burn + knockback),
  BACKDRAFT (burn + parry), RIME_TRAIL (slow + dash-through). Cascades
  are intentional.
- **Branching runs.** 7-room linear DAG with two 3-way choice forks
  (altar / vault / brave). Shrines heal, vaults gift legendaries,
  brave paths boost enemies and rewards.
- **Permanent progress.** Ether Shard currency persists across deaths
  — spend on Resilience, Quick Step, First Talisman, Tribute, or
  Bound Vow at the upgrade altar.

### Why Early Access

Ethera's core loop (combat, progression, room chemistry) is shipping
quality. Early Access is for the final 25% — content density,
audio polish, and balance from real-player telemetry. Mega Crit
called this the right balance for Slay the Spire; we agree.

### What's in (today)

- Single mage class, 7-room run, branching DAG with shrine + vault detours
- 21 enemies across 9 distinct AI patterns (chase / telegraphed melee /
  ranged / stationary / summoner / healer / bomber / wraith / glyph warden)
- 3 boss encounters (Iron Revenant, Broodmother, Ember Tyrant)
- 4 biomes (Crypt, Ossuary, Sanctuary, Ember) with unique music tracks
- 59 relics, 4 actives, 4 spell modifiers, 6 status combos
- Permanent meta-progression hub (5 upgrade nodes)
- Accessibility kit (screen-shake slider, separate music/SFX, motion
  reduction, text scale)

### What's NOT in (yet — beta roadmap)

- More biomes (floor 4 in development)
- More boss patterns (arena-mechanic, multi-entity)
- More enemy archetypes (flying, shield, charger)
- Authored prop art (chest / door / pedestal currently use restrained
  procedural geometry — biome storytelling carries the load)
- Composer audio pass (synthesized SFX placeholders → curated foley)
- Voice / narrator
- Steam achievements (12 in-game achievements unlock locally; Steam
  integration comes with EA tag)
- Cloud save / controller rebinding UI (controls work today; the
  GUI lands in beta v2)

### Pricing

Standard Steam EA discount expected for the EA period. Final pricing
announced near 1.0.
